5.2 HIV DRUG RESISTANCE TESTING

The country will for specified periods, collect data on early warning indicators for HIVDR. To routinely conduct HIV drug resistance surveys, the country will adopt the use of remnant viral load samples (plasma or DBS) for HIVDR survey activities.

1. Remnant blood samples of all clients with high viral load may be stored and used for surveillance purposes to guide program and policy changes.

2. Remnant blood samples of all infants who test HIV positive may be stored and used for surveillance purposes (plasma or DBS) for HIVDR survey activities.

To routinely estimate pretreatment drug resistance, HIVDR testing should be performed on all breakthrough infections among clients taking PrEP.

6.1.1 TB DIAGNOSIS

Clients (PLHIV) diagnosed with TB using TB LAM are classified as bacteriologically confirmed cases of TB. House-hold contacts of index cases diagnosed with TB using TB LAM are therefore eligible for TPT.

Fluid management:
- Volume status: hypovolemia: fluid bolus NaCl 0.9% 20 ml/kg and rehydration.
- Euvolemic: insensible losses (400 ml/m2 or 30 ml/kg/day) + urine and GI losses.
- Hypervolemia: insensible losses; furosemide 2-4 mg/kg.
- If nephrotic syndrome: consider 25% albumin 0.5 to 1 g/kg/dose over 30 to 60 (min), followed by a diuretic: Lasix 1-2 mg/kg.
Hypertension:
- Furosemide if fluid overload: IV, start 1 to 2 mg/kg/dose, 2nd dose 6 hours later (max 6 mg/kg).
- Calcium channel blockers: amlodipine oral 0.05 to 0.3 mg/kg/dose OD (max 0.6 mg/kg/dose or 10 mg).
- IV labetalol 0.2 to 1 mg/kg/dose if severe hypertension.
- N.B. Avoid ACEI & ARBs in AKI.
Electrolytes and acidosis:
- Hyperkalemia: 10% calcium gluconate 0.5-1 ml/kg over 10 min + glucose ('25-5 mls' of D10W, as printed) with insulin 0.1 IU/kg; +/- salbutamol nebulised 2.5 mg (<25 kg) and 5 mg (>25 kg); +/- sodium bicarbonate 1-2 mmol (ml)/kg over 10-30 min; / calcium resonium (kayexalate) 1 g/kg 4-hourly with oral lactulose 2.5 ml (<1 year), 5 ml (1-5 years), 10 ml (>5 years).
- Hyponatremia: if Na <118 mmol/l, calculate the sodium deficit: Na deficit = (125 - measured Na) x weight x 0.6; give over 2 hours as hypertonic saline 3%, target 125 mmol/l.
- Hypocalcemia: 10% calcium gluconate 0.1 mg/kg (0.5 ml/kg), as printed.
- Acidosis: 8.4% NaHCO3 deficit = (18 - measured HCO3) x 0.5 x weight, or NaHCO3 1-2 mmol/kg/dose TDS.
Nutrition: adequate nutrition is mandatory; from day 2 introduce protein gradually. Requirements: 95-115 kcal/kg and protein 1.5-2 g/kg ('1.5-21 g/kg' printed). Treat underlying causes.

Urgent dialysis if: hyperkalemia > 6.5 mmol/L ('605mmol/L' printed) not responding to medical treatment; severe fluid overload with pulmonary edema resistant to furosemide; urea > 40 mmol/L (30 mmol/L in neonates); severe hyponatremia (Na <120 mEq/L) or hypernatremia (Na >160 mEq/L) or severe acidosis pH <7.2 that cannot be corrected with HCO3 due to fluid overload, or refractory; multi-system failure; anticipation of prolonged oliguria. → Refer to tertiary renal replacement therapy. All referrals requiring urgent RRT must be discussed with the physician and the receiving facility before referral.
