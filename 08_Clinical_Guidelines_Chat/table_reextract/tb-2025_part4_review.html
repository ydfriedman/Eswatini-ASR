16.1 Community Referrals and Linkages (Special Considerations for the diagnosis of TB in children:)
Special Considerations for the diagnosis of TB in children:
• Write down a facility-to-facility referral form for the child
• Call the facility where the child will be referred to and schedule an appointment
• Appoint the child for follow-up in the referring facility appointment register
• After the day of the agreed date to visit the facility, call the facility to follow up if client has honoured the appointment
• Follow up with the caregiver if the child did not reach the health facility and reappoint in the facility appointment register
