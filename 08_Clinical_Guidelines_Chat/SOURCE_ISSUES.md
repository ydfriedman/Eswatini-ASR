# Source issues for clinical review

Problems found **in the guidelines themselves** while re-extracting tables and flowcharts. They are not chunking errors: the chunks reproduce what is printed. Each needs a clinician to confirm the intended content, and some may need to go back to the guideline authors.

Pages are PDF pages. "Item" is the `table_id` in the chunk set.
